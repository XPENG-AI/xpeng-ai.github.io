01 / Overview

Efficiency starts with what we preserve.

Deep audio encoders connect speech to language models, but reducing their depth can disrupt the representations that recognition depends on. X-AuT explores how to build compact audio encoders while preserving reliable recognition behavior.

Our approach combines progressive compression with knowledge transfer from a larger model, guiding a smaller speech system toward an effective balance of recognition quality and inference cost.
